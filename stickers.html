Print tips
  • Print on glossy vinyl or sticker paper for drops that survive the elements.
  • In the print dialog set Scale = 100% (Actual size) — do not pick “Fit to page,” it shrinks the art and breaks the QR.
  • Choose Best / High quality. QR stickers scan cleanly at 2″ and up.
