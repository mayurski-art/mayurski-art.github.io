Layers on this part

The base is the part's own crop: set it, then 🔒 it in the table. + Add layer lays a piece of any picture on top of it: pick the picture in the strip, move or resize (or ✂ snip) the yellow box on the picture, then drag the piece into place on the part here (corner dot, wheel, the Size slider or + − scale it; wheel over the picture zooms the snip; Alt+wheel turns, arrows nudge, Esc back to the base). ⇆ ⇅ flip a layer. 🧽 Eraser (E) rubs the selected layer out on the part view: Strength is how far one stroke fades it (100% erases fully), 🖌 Restore paints it back. Editing a layer never moves the base or another layer; 🔒 a layer to pin it too. Tick Writing when the piece has text, so it reads correctly on the gun's right side. Layers are shared by both sides.
