  • Read the blade: watch where they cock their saber back. That's the side the swing comes from.
  • Perfect block: guard aimed at that side. Costs no block points, and a swing right after is a fast counter.
  • Disarm: perfect block, then flick the mouse hard the other way just before the hit.
  • Feint: right click during your own windup cancels the swing.
  • Clash: swing into their swing, then mash attack.
  • A heavy swing breaks a guard on the wrong side. Out of block points, your guard breaks.
