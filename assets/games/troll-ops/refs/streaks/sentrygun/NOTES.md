# sentrygun: references

The Sentry Gun scorestreak uses the zombies kit's BO2-style sentry model
(`models/zk-turret.glb`). Its references, including the user's BO2 render,
are kept in `refs/arms/sentry/` (see the NOTES.md there).

- `streak-icons/sentrygun.png`: the streak card picture. Rendered from
  `zk-turret.glb` in Blender 5.2 EEVEE at 256 px with a transparent
  background, from the front three-quarter on the camera side (the side
  without the ammo box), to match `samturret.png`. No generated images.
